It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, including iron deficiency or anaemia. Food values are typical averages and real foods vary. Too much iron can be harmful, and some people (for example those with haemochromatosis, or who are pregnant) need specific advice. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before starting, stopping or changing an iron supplement, and never ignore professional medical advice because of something in the app. Keep iron supplements away from children: an overdose can be fatal to a young child.
